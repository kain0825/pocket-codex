# Electronic wooden fish sound

Based on **Woodblock-med.wav** by **hollandm**, published 2023-06-27.
Source: https://freesound.org/people/hollandm/sounds/692819/
License: CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The author describes a wooden stick striking a woodblock, recorded with AKG P220 with EQ/compression. This is recorded wooden percussion, not claimed to be a traditional round mokugyo recording.

Public high-quality MP3 preview used as the source. Converted to mono 16kHz; lowered pitch/speed to 0.8x, trimmed onset and 120ms body, applied restrained peak scaling and a tail fade. Candidate B was selected by the user. No additional synthetic oscillator or artificial room reverb is mixed in.

The selected PCM is stored in pocket_wood_sound.h. Original source page, preview, processing script and file hashes are retained in assets/audio and this source tree. Credit is preserved although CC0 does not require attribution.
